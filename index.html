How ResolveAI works

A transparent workflow from first report to verified closure.

🧠

01 · Understand & classify

Analyses the complaint text, identifies the issue type and estimates its urgency using a transparent demo rules engine.

🧭

02 · Prioritise & route

Recommends a priority, responsible department and next action so the right team can take ownership.

🔔

03 · Track & close

Maintains a timestamped status history and prepares a closure summary when an officer resolves the case.
